# Portrait, products and engineering

The portfolio opens with Muhammad Taha Bin Zaeem's name and authentic portrait. Graphite, warm white, copper and jade connect the pages; the education archive and original credentials retain lighter reading surfaces. Large product interfaces and documentary photographs carry the work. Generated illustrations remain atmospheric and open in their own clearly captioned viewers.

The cover is `app/components/portfolio-cover.tsx`; wording lives in `HOME_COVER` and its portrait is the first `HOME_INTRO.portraits` item. Its gallery uses that exact personal collection. The circuit field is a code-native decorative SVG, not an invented project screenshot. The cover's independent name, portrait, circuit and introductory copy layers share native scroll progress.

`app/book-data.ts` controls continuous-page palettes. Direct-route equivalents, the introduction and the compact index are in `app/portfolio-direction.css`. Chapter introductions are in `app/chapter-atmosphere.css`; project/research compositions are in `app/chapters-premium.css`; people, education, achievements and contact are in `app/sections-premium.css`. These modules preserve existing IDs, galleries and optional records.

The CPU architecture study is `app/components/cpu-architecture-study.tsx`. It appears only while the `vector-cpu` record exists in `PROJECTS`. It explains four 16-bit vector lanes, a 64-bit SIMD datapath and a custom 20-bit ISA. Its SVG is explicitly a simplified architectural illustration, not a simulation or a waveform. Its evidence anchor opens the same curated collection as the existing CPU project. Edit the diagram when the architectural facts change; remove the corresponding project to remove the study automatically.

Motion is an enhancement. Native scrolling remains authoritative; no wheel/touch interception or extra animation dependency is required. Quiet/reduced motion, ordinary reader flow, print and no-JavaScript retain the complete content and final diagrams. Measurements precede transforms, documentary images remain planar, and cleanup restores all temporary styles.
